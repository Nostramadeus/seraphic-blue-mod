Opening · Opening words — excerpt

Japanese source

始まりを否定せよ。 始まりの在らざる事を知れ。

Released English

Forget the beginning... And learn its non-existence.

Deny the beginning. Know that there is none.
What changes
  • The Japanese says deny, rather than forget.
  • The updated wording follows the project's fixed wording.

Opening · Opening poem — one stanza

Japanese source

眩しい日差しに眼を細め 無限に広がる蒼い空 終わりを知らぬ私の夢想が 息せき切らして翔けていた

Released English

Dazzling sunlight would shine on my eyes, streaming from the boundlessness above. Panting, I pushed myself to great heights, chasing after dreams I knew no end of.

Corrected English

Squinting in dazzling sunlight, beneath the boundless blue sky, my endless dreams flew breathlessly onward.
What changes
  • The dreams themselves fly breathlessly.
  • Restores the squinting eyes and blue sky.

One complete changed stanza, not the full poem. Japanese checked against an enlarged original; exact shipped English checked visually. Final test.7 wording, matched to the replacement manifest; rendering and publication checked separately.

Opening · Opening reflection — excerpt

Japanese source

何時からだろう。 人々は青空に、希望と自由の二つ名を与え、 事ある毎にその名を空に呼んでいた。 ふざけた話と自分は思う。 馬鹿の一つ覚えに青空を崇める、 愚かな悪習と自分は思う。

Released English

Since time immemorial, people have sent their prayers for hope and freedom to the heavens. With their minds cluttered by absurd words and foolish vices, they pour their problems out into the blue sky...

Corrected English

Since when, I wonder? People gave the blue sky two names, hope and freedom, and called those names to it at every turn. I find it absurd. A foolish habit: worshipping the sky with a mindless refrain.
What changes
  • People name the sky; they do not merely pray to it.
  • Restores the narrator's own critical judgment.

Exact opening excerpt of Ep00_Mes06. Later cards and alternative English ending cards are deliberately not combined with this excerpt. Both Japanese originals checked enlarged. Final test.7 wording, matched to the replacement manifest; rendering and publication checked separately.

After finishing Episode 2 · Episode 2 reflection — excerpt

Japanese source

だが何時からか、涙に取って代わった。 疑念に似て程遠い、心を黒く曇らせる情念。 俺以外に、此処に来るべき“奴”がいる。

Released English

But some time later, I held back my tears and changed. Now, I'm far from having doubts, and dark emotions quiver in my heart. Other than me, there's “another guy” who should be here.

Corrected English

But at some point, something took the place of my tears. A passion like doubt, yet far removed, clouding my heart black. Someone besides me ought to come here.
What changes
  • Another feeling replaces the tears; he does not choose to hold them back.
  • Keeps the heart becoming dark.

After finishing Episode 2 · Episode 2: the work — excerpt

Japanese source

そう、イーヴルスイーパーをやる理由。 それが半分は趣味で在る事の理由。 イーヴルを殺す事。 それは一つの稼業、一つの正義として認められ、 大手を振って堂々と、何にも憚らずに為せる業。

Released English

Yes, this is why I became a Devil Sweeper. This is why my job's partly a hobby of mine. Killing Devils is recognized by all as a righteous job. It is a job in which I can do magnificent things without any hesitation.

Corrected English

Yes, that is why I work as an Evil Sweeper. Why the work is half a hobby. Killing Evils. Recognized as a trade, as justice, something I can do openly and proudly, with no restraint.
What changes
  • Uses Evil and Evil Sweeper, the agreed creature and job names.
  • The Japanese means acting openly and proudly, not doing magnificent things.

Exact first two cards of Ep02_Mes02, not its full five-card passage. Japanese originals and exact shipped English visually checked. Literary narration keeps the short fragments. Final test.7 wording, matched to the replacement manifest; rendering and publication checked separately.

After finishing Episode 2 · Episode 2: a question

Japanese source

だがな、イーヴルさんよ。 若し出来る事なら、 俺はあんた達に一つ聞きたい事が有る。 生まれて来て、幸せだったか？

Released English

But anyway, Mr. Devil... If you're able to talk, there's one thing I wanna ask you. Are you happy you came into this world?

Corrected English

But listen, Mr. Evil. If it were possible, there is one thing I would ask all of you. Are you happy you came into this world?
What changes
  • Uses the agreed name Evil.
  • Keeps the plural address and the source's open condition.
