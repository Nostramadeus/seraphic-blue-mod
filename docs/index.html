Seraphic Blue: translation comparison

Japanese script, the 2016 English patch, and this project's English, side by side. 429 dialogue boxes across the whole game, plus 29 boxes in a separate section on softened lines.

Spoiler warning. This page quotes dialogue from the whole game, including late scenes. The spoiler setting below hides later parts, but if you want to play blind, close this page now and come back after the ending.

What this is. Seraphic Blue (RPG Maker 2000, 2004-2005) got its first English translation in 2016, which made the game playable in English for ten years. This project translated the script again from the Japanese, line by line, and compared every box with the 2016 wording. The page shows a sample of where the two differ and says, in plain words, what the Japanese says and what each English version did with it.

Columns. Japanese = the original box. 2016 patch = the first English translation. This translation = the text in the current release. Why = what the difference is, written for a reader who knows no Japanese. Tag "Both fine" marks boxes where both versions are fair and this one is simply a different choice.

How boxes were picked. A script listed every dialogue box where the two English versions differ in wording (about 16,000 of 24,500 boxes). For each part of the story it kept the 90 most different plus 40 at random. Reviewers (Claude Opus 5.5, directed by Claude Fable 5.1) read the Japanese for each candidate, kept about 40 per part that show the difference clearly, and wrote the fourth column. Any candidate where this translation was found weaker was left out of the page and logged for fixing.
